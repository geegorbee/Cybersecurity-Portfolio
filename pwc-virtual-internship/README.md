# PwC Cybersecurity & Controls Virtual Internship – Case Study Portfolio

This project summarizes my completed work for the **PwC Cybersecurity & Controls Virtual Internship**, including analysis, professional communication, control testing, and findings presentation.
All scenarios and exemplar responses have been **summarized in my own words** out of respect for PwC’s intellectual property.
This portfolio focuses on my reasoning, analysis, and growth as a cybersecurity professional.

## 🧠 Key Skills Demonstrated

### 🔹 Process & Controls Analysis
- Evaluated Procure-to-Pay processes for SOX readiness
- Identified segregation of duties, access control, and financial process gaps
- Assessed missing business process, ITGC, and automated controls

### 🔹 SDLC & IT Governance
- Compared MedTech’s SDLC framework to NIST standards
- Identified missing design reviews and undocumented configuration management
- Developed targeted walkthrough questions and led clarifying discussions

### 🔹 Change Management Testing
- Documented Test of Design (ToD) and Test of Operating Effectiveness (ToE)
- Identified gaps in emergency change approvals
- Detected segregation of duties conflicts (developer vs implementer roles)

### 🔹 Communication & Presentation
- Drafted professional email summaries and risk analyses
- Created executive-level summary slides
- Applied PwC-style clarity and structure in reporting

## 📝 Lessons Learned

- How to analyze a process and identify control gaps
- How to assess risks and connect them to business impacts
- The importance of segregation of duties in financial and IT processes
- Value of reviewing access and controls periodically
- How to structure walkthroughs and extract meaningful evidence
- How to compare and refine work based on professional exemplars
