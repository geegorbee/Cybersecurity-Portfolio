## Completed Projects

### [PwC Cybersecurity & Controls](./PwC-Cyber/)

**Focus:** Enterprise risk assessment, SOX compliance, IT general controls  
**Completion:** November 2025  
**Platform:** Forage

Conducted comprehensive cybersecurity risk assessment for healthcare startup preparing for IPO. Analyzed procure-to-pay processes, SDLC controls, and change management procedures. Identified critical control gaps including segregation of duties violations and access control weaknesses. Documented Test of Design and Operating Effectiveness for IT general controls with formal exception reporting.

**Key Skills:** GRC, SOX compliance, RBAC, segregation of duties, audit walkthroughs, control testing, executive communication

**Deliverables:**

- Procure-to-Pay risk assessment with 6 critical findings
- SDLC security controls analysis against NIST SP 800-64
- Change management control testing (ToD/ToE) with 2 exceptions documented
- Executive findings summary with 90-day remediation roadmap

## Why Virtual Internships?

Virtual internships provide:

- **Real-world scenarios** based on actual company challenges
- **Employer-branded credentials** recognized by industry
- **Practical deliverables** for portfolio demonstration
- **Diverse skill exposure** across multiple security domains
- **Structured learning paths** with clear objectives and outcomes

These programs simulate authentic consulting and analyst work, including client documentation review, stakeholder communication, technical analysis, and professional reporting—core competencies for cybersecurity roles.
